Beat four · why a registry cares

Auction replaces the discount.

A
Markdown

Cut the price until something moves. Margin goes first, the name still carries no story, and you learn nothing about what a buyer would actually have paid.

B
Auction

Demand sets the price. Every closed auction is a real willingness-to-pay data point, per TLD, per region — and the board gives people a reason to come back tomorrow.

Price discovery, not price cutting Live demand signal per TLD A daily reason to return
What's next

Continuous runs

The pool is designed to be refilled, not rebuilt. Candidates survive their keyword going quiet, and durability finally starts to mean something.
