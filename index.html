Where this should sit

The intelligence is the product. The inventory is not.

What we are not proposing

A registry registering trending names ahead of the market is warehousing, whatever it is priced at. Holding inventory you created the demand for is bad faith, and an auction run on it has a compliance problem before it has a business problem.

What it actually is

A domain-centric Google Trends. Keyword velocity by region, semantically matched to names that are still free, screened for trademark and brandability, linking out to buy. The registry never touches the name; the buyer registers it themselves.

What Radix gets

Demand data available nowhere else: which keyword and extension pairings people actually chase, by region, by day. That feeds pricing, marketing and portfolio decisions, with no inventory held and nothing to defend.

Whatever the internet is talking about tomorrow is already a domain.

Somebody just has to find it before the trend does.
